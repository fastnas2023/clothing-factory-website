Fabric Knowledge Base

Common Fabric Categories & Characteristics

From natural fibers to functional fabrics, understand the characteristics and use cases of different materials for professional product development and sourcing decisions.

Cotton Fabrics

Breathable, moisture-absorbing and skin-friendly; ideal for daily basics such as T-shirts, shirts and loungewear. Organic cotton and blended options available.

View Details →

Polyester & Blends

Wrinkle-resistant, durable and colorfast; often blended with cotton and spandex. Suitable for sportswear, workwear and fast-fashion bulk orders.

View Details →

Knit Fabrics

High elasticity and fit; covers single jersey, interlock, rib and more, widely used for hoodies, T-shirts and base layers.

View Details →

Functional Fabrics

Waterproof, quick-dry, antimicrobial, UV-protective and cooling functions to meet outdoor sports, professional workwear and special-scenario needs.

View Details →

Denim Fabrics

From lightweight stretch denim to heavy raw denim, with washing, fading and whiskering processes for pants, jackets and skirts.

View Details →

Linen Fabrics

Natural, eco-friendly, cool and breathable with a unique texture; often used for summer shirts, pants and premium casualwear.

View Details →
